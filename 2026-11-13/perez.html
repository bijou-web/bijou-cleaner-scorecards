Data note (60 days): RapidEye findings readable for 11 of 23 cleans.

How scored (same as v3). Each clean earns up to 100: Final QC 40 (Passed and RapidEye shows no Moderate/Critical = 40 · Passed by reviewer but RapidEye has Moderate/Critical findings or no readable report = 20 · Pending or blank = 0, it counts as not passed · Failed = 0) + Timing 30 (finished by 2 PM local = 30 · 2–6 PM = 15 · after 6 PM or no finish time = 0) + Cleaning 30 (30 minus 5 per Minor and 10 per Moderate/Critical cleaning miss from the RapidEye report; maintenance/damage is listed but not counted). When a part has no data for a clean (no readable RapidEye report, or no timing data before Sep 15) that part is left out and the clean is scored on the parts we have, scaled to 100. Arrows compare with the previous period of the same length; rank is among cleaners with cleans in that period.
What the data supports: RapidEye findings are read from the report PDFs saved on our side; the earliest is Oct 5, so there are no RapidEye findings before Oct 5 and cleaning misses only exist for recent cleans. Timing evidence starts Sep 15 and is thin before Oct 19 (many "unknown"). History before Nov 1 comes from the old Cleaners QC board (archived, read-only export Nov 13), Nov 1+ from the new board; the same clean on both boards is counted once. Read the 30- and 60-day arrows with care: most of the rise is because the old board marked many Aug–Sep cleans Failed or left them blank, while Oct–Nov cleans were mostly marked Passed by reviewers. The earlier periods also have little or no timing and findings data. The arrows show the scores moved up; they do not prove the cleaning got better. Last week's v2 report used a different method, so its numbers are not comparable.
